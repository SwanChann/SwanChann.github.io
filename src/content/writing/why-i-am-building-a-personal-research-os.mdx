Research work rarely fails because there is nowhere to type. It becomes fragile when questions, sources, experiments, code, decisions, and conclusions live in separate places with no durable relationship.

## The problem is continuity

A research notebook is useful for recording the day. A reference manager is useful for storing papers. A repository is useful for code. None of them alone preserves why an experiment was chosen, which evidence changed a decision, or what remains uncertain.

A personal Research OS is a working convention across those tools. Its job is to preserve continuity:

- a question can lead to sources;
- sources can lead to a testable claim;
- a claim can lead to an experiment;
- an experiment can update the project state;
- a stable lesson can become public writing.

## Markdown as the durable layer

Markdown is not the entire system. It is the smallest durable layer that both a person and an AI agent can inspect, diff, and maintain without a proprietary database.

```text
capture → organize → connect → refine → publish
```

This website follows that same path. Notes are allowed to stay small. Experience records solved problems. Research pages hold evolving questions. Projects preserve complete project stories. Writing is where a more complete view emerges.
